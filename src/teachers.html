For teachers

Computer science doesn't have to be intimidating to teach.

You don't need a computer science background or a perfect lesson plan to begin. Start with one familiar idea, make the thinking visible, and build confidence one step at a time.

A simple way to start

  1. Begin with something familiar: instructions, patterns, or sorting.
  2. Name the computing idea students are already using.
  3. Let students test, change, and explain what happens.

Start small, then build

Computer science can begin away from a screen. Try a short activity, use the teaching notes to surface common misunderstandings, then connect the idea to code when your class is ready.
